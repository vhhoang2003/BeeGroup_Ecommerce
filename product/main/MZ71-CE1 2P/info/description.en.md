# GIGABYTE MZ71-CE1 2P

**Dual-socket SP3 server board: 2 × EPYC, 16 DIMM slots, 5 SlimSAS ports and built-in IPMI management.**

The GIGABYTE MZ71-CE1 runs two AMD EPYC processors for up to 128 cores on one board. 16 DDR4 slots (8 channels per CPU), up to five PCIe x16 slots, five SlimSAS connectors for SATA or NVMe drives and an ASPEED AST2500 BMC with a dedicated management port make it a proven foundation for render farms, virtualization clusters and storage servers.

**Price:** 21.944.000 VND

## Highlights

- Dual SP3 – up to 2 × 64 cores
- 16 × DDR4 slots, 8 channels per CPU
- 5 × SlimSAS + 4 × SATA + M.2
- ASPEED AST2500 BMC with IPMI
- Official GIGABYTE server platform

## Notes

- CPU support depends on board revision: rev 1.x supports EPYC 7001/7002; EPYC 7003 (Zen 3) requires rev 3.x or later. Check the revision sticker before selling with Milan CPUs.
- E-ATX (305 × 330 mm) – needs a case that supports E-ATX / SSI-EEB.
