# HUANANZHI H12D-8D 1P

**Single-socket SP3 board for EPYC 7002/7003 with 4 × PCIe 4.0 x16, 3 × M.2 NVMe and dual 2.5 GbE in a standard ATX size.**

The HUANANZHI H12D-8D brings EPYC to a regular ATX case. Eight DDR4 channels (up to 2 TB), four full-length PCIe 4.0 x16 slots for GPUs or NVMe cards, three M.2 NVMe slots and three SFF-8643 ports for up to 12 extra SATA drives or U.2 SSDs make it a great base for home labs, render boxes and AI workstations.

**Price:** 11.710.400 VND

## Highlights

- SP3, EPYC 7002/7003
- 8-channel DDR4, up to 2 TB
- 4 × PCIe 4.0 x16
- 3 × M.2 NVMe + 3 × SFF-8643
- Dual Intel 2.5 GbE, ATX size

## Notes

- This version has no BMC: no IPMI remote management and the rear VGA port is inactive – you need a graphics card for display output.
