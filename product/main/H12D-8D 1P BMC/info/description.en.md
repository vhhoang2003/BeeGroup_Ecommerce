# HUANANZHI H12D-8D 1P BMC

**H12D-8D with the BMC module installed: IPMI 2.0 remote management, onboard VGA and full fan control.**

Same platform as the H12D-8D – EPYC 7002/7003, 8-channel DDR4 up to 2 TB, 4 × PCIe 4.0 x16, 3 × M.2 NVMe and dual 2.5 GbE – plus the ASPEED AST2500 BMC. You get a dedicated management port for IPMI 2.0 (remote power, console and monitoring), working onboard VGA, and BMC-controlled system fans. The right choice for headless servers and racks.
