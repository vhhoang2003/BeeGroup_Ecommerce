# GL.iNet Comet Pro (GL-RM10)

**Premium GL.iNet KVM over IP: Wi-Fi 6, 2.22-inch touchscreen and 4K HDMI loop-through.**

The GL.iNet Comet Pro (GL-RM10) is the step-up Comet for KVM that has to work anywhere: Wi-Fi 6 next to Gigabit Ethernet (with automatic failover between the two), a 2.22-inch touchscreen for the IP address and quick setup, and an HDMI output so the local monitor keeps working while you control the machine remotely. Video goes up to 4K@30fps with hardware H.264 encoding, plus 32 GB eMMC and power control through an ATX board or Fingerbot.

## Highlights

- Wi-Fi 6 + Gigabit Ethernet with failover
- 2.22-inch touchscreen
- HDMI loop-through, video up to 4K@30fps
- 32 GB eMMC
- BIOS/UEFI-level control

## Specifications

- **Model:** GL-RM10 (Comet Pro)
- **CPU:** Quad-core ARM Cortex-A53
- **Memory / storage:** 1 GB DDR3L / 32 GB eMMC
- **Operating system:** Linux 6.1
- **Display:** 2.22-inch touchscreen
- **Video:** HDMI in + HDMI out (loop-through), up to 4K @ 30 fps
- **Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi 6 (802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax), 286 Mbps 2.4 GHz + 286 Mbps 5 GHz
- **Ethernet:** Gigabit, automatic failover between Wi-Fi and Ethernet
- **Ports:** USB-C (keyboard / mouse), USB 2.0 Type-A (ATX board / Fingerbot), Gigabit Ethernet, HDMI in, HDMI out, USB-C power
- **Power input:** USB-C 5 V / 2 A, PD compatible
- **Dimensions / weight:** 93 × 84 × 47 mm / 170 g
