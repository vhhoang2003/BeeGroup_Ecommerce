# NVIDIA A2 16GB (Tesla A2)

**Low-profile 60 W Ampere data-center GPU with 16 GB GDDR6 for AI inference, video transcoding and vGPU.**

The NVIDIA A2 packs 16 GB of GDDR6, 3rd-gen Tensor cores and RT cores into a single-slot, low-profile card that draws only 40–60 W from the PCIe slot – no extra power cable needed. It is built for entry-level AI inference, intelligent video analytics, Plex/Jellyfin transcoding (with AV1 decode) and virtual desktops in compact servers.

**Price:** 11.440.000 VND

## Highlights

- 16 GB GDDR6, 200 GB/s
- Ampere: 1280 CUDA, 40 Tensor, 10 RT cores
- Only 40–60 W, no external power
- Single-slot low-profile, PCIe 4.0 x8
- AV1 decode, NVIDIA vGPU support

## Notes

- Passively cooled: in a desktop case add a fan blowing directly through the card, or it will overheat and throttle.
- * Tensor values with structural sparsity. 'Tesla' is the legacy brand name – NVIDIA's official name is NVIDIA A2.
